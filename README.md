# 📊 Business Intelligence Dashboards (Power BI)

This repository contains interactive dashboards developed using Microsoft Power BI, focused on analyzing business performance, marketing effectiveness, and healthcare outcomes.

## 🚀 Projects

### 1. Marketing Channel Effectiveness Dashboard
- Analyzed the impact of TV, Radio, and Newspaper advertising on sales performance
- Built KPI dashboards showing total spend and total sales (~$2.80bn)
- Conducted correlation analysis between marketing spend and revenue
- Key insights:
  - TV drives high sales but at high cost
  - Radio is the most cost-effective channel (strong ROI)
  - Newspaper ads show low effectiveness and should be reduced
- Provided recommendations for optimizing marketing budget allocation

---

### 2. Staffing and Care Outcomes in Long-Term Care
- Analyzed healthcare staffing data (LPN, CNA, contractor vs full-time)
- Examined relationship between staffing levels and patient outcomes
  (rehospitalization and emergency visits)
- Built interactive dashboard with provider-level comparisons
- Key insights:
  - Understaffing leads to worse patient outcomes
  - Contractor staffing improves care quality and flexibility
- Provided data-driven recommendations for operational and staffing strategy
